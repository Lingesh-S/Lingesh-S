### About Me

Data Scientist at **DataTracks**, building AI/ML systems that automate financial reporting workflows.

- **Generative AI & LLMs** — fine-tuning language models (LoRA / PEFT) for domain tasks such as intelligent **XBRL tagging** and MCQ generation
- **RAG & AI Agents** — retrieval pipelines, LangChain / LangGraph assistants, and multi-agent orchestration
- **Automation** — end-to-end workflows with n8n, FastAPI services, and containerised deployments
- **Computer Vision** — CNN-based medical image classification with Grad-CAM explainability
- Open to collaboration on **open-source AI**, **LLM tooling**, and **automation** projects
